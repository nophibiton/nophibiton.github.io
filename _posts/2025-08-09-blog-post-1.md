Excited to share that I’ve started my postdoctoral research at the University of Sao Paulo (USP)! 🎉

⭐I’ll be working with [Prof. Andre Beck](https://scholar.google.com.tw/citations?user=lEZpIvgAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao) in the Escola de Engenharia de São Carlos, USP, focusing on the risk-based integrated structural design of structural systems considering fatigue-initiated progressive collapse where uncertainties in both technical and non-technical factors are explicitly considered.

📝This research builds on my previous work in reliability-based design optimization and structural system reliability, and I’m looking forward to expanding these methods to improve risk-based life cycle assessment of fatigue prone structures.

🙏 Grateful for the opportunity funded by [FAPESP Postdoc Fellowship](https://fapesp.br/en/postdoc) and be able to collaborate with such a talented team, and eager to see where this next chapter in my academic journey will lead.
